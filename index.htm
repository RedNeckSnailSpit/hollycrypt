// hollycrypt

Decryption animation visualiser

root@rednecksnailspit:~$ ./reveal --demo
What is this?
A visual demo of what a decryption reveal animation could look like in a real app — where data is stored encrypted at rest and only revealed to you once you've proven ownership, animating from the raw encrypted/hashed value down to your actual plaintext. Add some fields below and watch the magic.
